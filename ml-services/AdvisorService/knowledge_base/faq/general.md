## What is the difference between the five career clusters?
The platform groups IT jobs into five clusters: Software Engineering (building applications), DevOps/Cloud (deploying and running systems), Cybersecurity (protecting systems), Networking & Infrastructure (connecting and maintaining systems) and Data & AI (working with data and models).

## What is QA?
QA means quality assurance: checking that software works as intended by designing tests, running them and reporting problems. QA engineers may test by hand or write automated tests.

## How do I choose a cluster?
Try one small project in two or three clusters and notice which work you enjoy. Look at the skills that postings ask for and compare them with the modules you have already taken.

## Do I need a certification?
It depends on the cluster and the employer. Certifications can structure your study but projects and clear evidence of skills matter too. (verify with research data)
